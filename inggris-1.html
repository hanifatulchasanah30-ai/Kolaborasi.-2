Alliya Zahrani Izzia Fathin

General Statement An upcycled keyboard heart artwork is a unique piece of creative home decor that transforms discarded computer parts into a meaningful visual statement. By merging technology with canvas painting, this artwork serves as an innovative example of modern sustainable art.

Explanation sequence

  1. Keycap Collection: The process begins by gathering unused or broken computer keyboards. The individual black keycaps are carefully detached, cleaned, and sorted according to their sizes and shapes.
  2. Heart Shape Formation: Before applying any paint to the background, the collected keycaps are organized and laid out directly onto the canvas to form a heart shape. This initial arrangement ensures that the proportions, spacing, and composition of the heart are balanced before securing them in place.
  3. Background coloring: Once the heart structure is mapped out and secured, the background of the canvas is painted using acrylics. Vibrant sunset hues-such as soft pink, yellow, and orange gradient,are blended alongside a landscape at the bottom to create a striking contrast around the keyboard pieces.
  4. Final Inspection: The last step is a thorough inspection of the finished artwork. This quality check ensures that all keycaps are securely attached, the paint edges around the heart are clean, and the final mixed-media piece is ready for display.

Closing

This upcycled piece not only elevates interior spaces with a touch of modern tech aesthetic, but also highlights how everyday electronic waste can be skillfully transformed into valuable art through a thoughtful, step-by-step creative process.

Muhammad Alva Naja Izzani

General Statement Over time, waste and trash continue to accumulate—a phenomenon that can occur anywhere, including in computer labs. For this reason, we should repurpose the waste found in such environments. Take keyboards, for example: we can reuse old, discarded keyboards by transforming them into something useful—such as the pixel art wall decor we offer. Our product is crafted from repurposed keyboard keys, which are cleaned and arranged to form specific images or designs.

The Transformation Process The process of creating this pixel art wall decoration involves only two steps, as it is quite simple to make:

  1. Removing the keys from a keyboard
  2. Cleaning the keyboard keys

The Formation of a New Product Once the keyboard keys have been removed and cleaned, we can begin creating the wall decoration. This process is quite challenging, as it requires both precision and patience. The steps are as follows:

  1. Create a sketch of the desired image.
  2. Construct a frame and a backing that suit the image.
  3. Attach the keyboard keys according to the sketch.
  4. Apply color to the keys to match the design.
  5. Fit the frame onto the piece and display it on the wall.

The Result and Its Impact The result is a beautiful wall decoration that is pleasing to the eye; furthermore, this activity helps reduce waste and fosters individual creativity.

Hanifatul CHasanah

General Overview Electronic waste and old keyboard parts are often discarded and overlooked, yet they hold hidden potential. Many people simply throw away outdated or broken keyboards, contributing to growing piles of waste that harm our environment. However, with creativity and effort, these unwanted items can be transformed into something beautiful and meaningful.

The Transformation Process The process starts by collecting keys from unused or broken keyboards, cleaning them carefully to remove dust and dirt. Next, arrange and fit the keys together to form a specific shape or design—such as a heart, symbol, or pattern—working piece by piece until the form is complete. Meanwhile, prepare a canvas or board and paint it with a colorful background that complements the arrangement, adding depth and beauty to the overall look.
